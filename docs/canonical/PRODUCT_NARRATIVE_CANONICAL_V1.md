## Canonical One-Line Narrative

> CoCalLab turns messy carbon evidence into reviewed, traceable report packages.

## Canonical Positioning

CoCalLab is a collaborative evidence-to-report workspace for teams preparing carbon inventory reports.

It helps users:

- Collect carbon evidence.
- Review extracted data before it becomes inventory.
- Turn reviewed evidence into emissions activities.
- Understand numbers through activity/calculation context.
- Generate report packages that can be discussed and reviewed.

## What Problem CoCalLab Solves

Carbon reporting usually starts in messy places:

- Email attachments.
- Electricity bills.
- Fuel receipts.
- Procurement records.
- Department spreadsheets.
- Consultant checklists.
- Repeated review questions.

The painful question is:

> How do we get from this evidence mess to a report someone can understand?

CoCalLab solves that workflow problem.

## What CoCalLab Is Not

CoCalLab should not be described as:

- A complete ESG command center.
- A formal compliance validation engine.
- An audit-ready certification platform.
- A CBAM-ready product.
- A Product CFP/LCA engine.
- A full legal entity/site governance system.
- A fully automated carbon accounting replacement for human review.

These may be future ambitions or separate roadmap items, but they are not the canonical current product narrative.

## Core Narrative

Teams preparing carbon reports are buried in evidence, spreadsheets, and review questions.

CoCalLab helps them turn that evidence into reviewed emissions activities and traceable report packages, so they can spend less time rebuilding spreadsheets and more time explaining the numbers with confidence.

The value is not "more modules." The value is the visible path:

> Evidence -> Draft -> Activity -> Analytics -> Report

The product wins when a user can say:

> I can see how this bill became this activity, how this activity affected the total, and how that total ended up in the report.

## 30-Second Narrative

Most carbon inventory work starts with scattered bills, spreadsheets, and review questions.

CoCalLab helps teams turn that messy evidence into reviewed carbon activity data and then into a traceable report package. The value is simple: less retyping, fewer unclear numbers, and a clearer path from source document to report.

## 3-Minute Narrative

Carbon inventory work rarely starts in a clean system. It starts with bills, receipts, spreadsheets, and people asking what data is still missing.

That creates three problems:

- Teams waste time retyping and reorganizing evidence.
- Reviewers do not trust numbers without context.
- Reports are often disconnected from the work that produced them.

CoCalLab is built around a simpler story:

> Evidence in, reviewed activities out, report ready for discussion.

A user starts with source evidence. CoCalLab helps turn that evidence into draft data. A human reviews the draft before it becomes an emissions activity. Those activities support analytics and report generation. The important part is the chain: document, draft, activity, calculation context, report.

That is what makes the product valuable. Not because it has many modules, but because it helps a team get from messy evidence to a report package they can explain.

## Primary Buyer Hypothesis

The first likely paid customer is an ESG consultant or small consulting team.

Reason:

- They already sell the work CoCalLab accelerates.
- Faster evidence-to-report preparation creates margin.
- Repeatable client workflows create account expansion potential.
- They can tolerate guided onboarding if report-package trust is strong enough.

Second likely segment:

- Accounting Firm, if reviewability and report caveats are excellent.

- CBAM is not a current workflow.
- Product CFP is not a current workflow.
- Legal Entity/Site governance is not a closed-beta workflow.
